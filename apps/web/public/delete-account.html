Delete Account

You can delete your El Cambio account and associated data at any time.

Delete in the app (fastest)

Mobile: Open the app and go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and custom rates immediately.

Web/PWA: Open the web app (cambio.sneyderangulo.com) and go to Settings → Delete account.

Delete by email

If you can’t access the app, email us at sneyder2328@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account and include the email address used to sign in. We will process email requests within 30 days.

Data deleted

  • Account information (Firebase Auth user)
  • Custom exchange rates linked to your account
